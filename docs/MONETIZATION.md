# FINNest — monetization and validation plan

This is a **pricing hypothesis**, not proof that customers will pay. Product must provide meaningful free value before asking for payment.

| Tier | Indicative price | Access in release |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Free | ₹0 | Limited statement imports, basic dashboard, transaction review, monthly summary |
| Plus | ₹999/year | Multi-account imports, longer history, recurring-charge analysis, Ask My Money, anomaly candidates, richer reports |
| One-time Tax Pack | ₹499/use | Missing-document checklist and CA-ready financial package, **not** tax filing |
| Tax Pro | ₹1,999/year | Later phase after professionally validated tax calculations and tax-intelligence features |
| CA Workspace | To be tested | V2 B2B subscription after real CA workflow pilots |

## Measurement

- Activation: first successful, reconciled upload + reviewed transactions.
- Leading retention metric: users upload a second statement in the following month.
- Revenue: conversion to paid, annual renewal, payment success, refund and chargeback rates.
- Unit economics: gross sales, taxes, payment processing, inference/document processing, hosting, support, acquisition and professional services.
- Explicitly measure how much a user trusts the figures and whether a CA saves time using the package.

## Sales boundaries

- No sale of user financial data or undisclosed financial-product targeting.
- No investment recommendations, direct tax filing, paid brokerage commissions or implicit CA fee splitting in V1.
- Price tests, receipts/tax invoices, refund policy and payment/merchant approvals need owner review before launch.
- Do not call revenue "profit" unless all operating, acquisition, refund and tax costs are accounted for.
