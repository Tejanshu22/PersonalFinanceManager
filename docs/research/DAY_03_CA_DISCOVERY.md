Suggested 30-minute route: 0–3 consent/context; 3–16 current process and recent generalized incident; 16–23 package/trust discussion; 23–27 neutral concept and optional commercial questions; 27–30 confirm understanding, gaps and follow-up permission. Follow the concrete story rather than forcing all probes. Record not-asked/declined separately from no problem. Never announce survey counts as an expected answer.

| Ref | Open-ended question | Neutral follow-up |

| Q01 | How do salaried clients currently send you documents, from first contact to a usable file set? | Describe a recent typical process without identifying a client. What already works well? |
| Q02 | Which documents or pieces of context, if any, tend to be missing? | How do you decide a request applies to that client and period? What can wait until later? |
| Q03 | Where does document administration take the most effort? | What happened last time? How often? Approximate time is optional; avoid identifiable case amounts |
| Q04 | How do you resolve an unexplained receipt, transfer or investment-related entry? | What do you ask the client, and what requires your judgment? What if the client cannot answer? |

| Q06 | Which automation errors would be unacceptable in preparation software? | How would you detect or recover from them? What should software refuse to infer? |
| Q07 | How do clients prepare during the year today, and does timing affect your work? | What benefit or extra work would year-round preparation create? |
| Q08 | How does document access and sharing work now? | What problems, if any, would time-limited selected access solve? What copy-retention needs remain after access ends? |
| Q09 | What would make you trust or reject a new document-preparation platform? | After spontaneous answers, probe source traceability, independent review, permissions, revocation limits and period correctness |
