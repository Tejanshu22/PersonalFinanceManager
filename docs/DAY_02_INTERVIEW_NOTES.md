## Common recording rules

- Mark each response as **S** (participant statement traceable to real notes), **O** (researcher observation of an action actually observed), **I** (researcher interpretation), or **U** (untested assumption). S means verified against the supplied interview record, not independently proven financial truth.
- Distinguish spontaneous, probed, concept-prompted and price-prompted answers. A narrated past event is S, not O. A hypothetical future choice is S with hypothetical scope, never payment/conversion evidence.
- Use `Not asked`, `Skipped`, `Not applicable`, `Declined`, `Unclear` or `Not supplied` as appropriate. A blank is not a negative answer.
- Frequency and severity are participant-described first. If the researcher adds a severity interpretation, label it I and explain its basis. Do not fabricate a numerical scale or cost.
- Quote only exact words captured with permission; label paraphrases without quotation marks. Indicate redactions with brackets and retain meaning. Omit an identifying quote rather than attempting unsafe redaction.
- Evidence IDs such as P01-E01 are research references, not new FIN/DAY feature IDs. Keep IDs stable and append corrections transparently. Keep contradictions, qualification and uncertainty.
- Proposed usable-interview criterion: one distinct consenting participant, a documented actual session, substantive current-behavior evidence, opportunity to discuss trust and value, and privacy-cleared notes. Record any topic gaps; do not force answers. A declined/skipped pricing question is not failure or evidence of unwillingness to pay.
- The user confirms distinctness using a private recruitment record; no identity proof belongs here. Withdrawal removes affected notes/evidence links under the agreed policy and changes the usable count. Do not recycle participant IDs to hide a withdrawal or incomplete interview.
