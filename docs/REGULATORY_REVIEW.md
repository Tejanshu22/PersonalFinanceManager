## Dates and applicability

The DPDP Act is the **2023 Act**, not a new “2025 Act.” Its commencement notification is dated **13 November 2025**. Immediate provisions primarily establish the framework/Board; section 6(9), section 27(1)(d) and Rules 4 phase in after one year (**13 November 2026**); core processing/rights provisions and Rules 3, 5–16, 22–23 after eighteen months (**13 May 2027**). Rules 1, 2, 17–21 commenced immediately. The Gazette issue is dated 13 November although its electronic identifier/press publication includes 14 November. The later official PIB statement expressly uses 13 May 2027. Counsel must confirm operative notifications/corrigenda and any subsequent changes before launch. [L1–L3]

As of this research date, the later phases are not yet in force. This does **not** establish a privacy/security exemption: counsel must assess the existing IT Act/SPDI framework and transition, contractual duties and other applicable laws. Do not assume “startup” creates an automatic exemption. [L1, L4]
